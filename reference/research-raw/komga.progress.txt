Reading progress is tracked for each user separately. Unread and in progress indicators ​ The book card will display read progress indicators: Unread books will display an orange tick in the top right corner: In progress books will display a progress bar at the bottom of the thumbnail: In addition, the series card will display the number of unread books in the top right corner: Marking progress manually ​ You can mark books or series as read or unread manually. Look for the action menu icon and click on Mark as read or Mark as unread .
