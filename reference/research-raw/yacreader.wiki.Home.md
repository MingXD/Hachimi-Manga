## What is YACReader?
YACReader is a comic reader and manager for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and other platforms.

## What Does YACReader Do?
It allows you to display digital comics on whatever platform you choose. Issues are typically compressed into a zipped file containing images of each comic page. YACReader presents this zipped file on your screen as you would typically read a physical comic book with navigation options and more.

## How To Know If YACReader Is Right For You?
Have you ever said the following statements to yourself or others?
* I wish I had a way to read my comics on my PC without relying on a web viewer.
* I have a comic reader I use now, but it hasn't been updated or worked on in years!
* My comics are all over the place! I need a good organizer for them.
* I use multiple operating systems. It'd be nice if I could have the same comic reader application on all of them.
* I'd sure love to find a good open source comic reader application to support!

Even if you've never said any of the above, if you have digital comics and need a way to read them - Give YACReader a try! It's actively maintained by comic lovers just like you. It's very easy to install on most platforms. Check out our [Installation Guide] and our [Quick-Start Guide] to jump in and see why its so loved!
